Equations, interpretation & numerical method

The state is (θ, ω). At each point the vector field is (ω, −9.81 sin θ − 2γω). The same state drives both panels. Arrows are normalized after conversion to screen coordinates: their direction is meaningful, but their length does not encode speed. The gold arrow beside the bob indicates its tangential velocity direction.

The full nonlinear equation is used, without a small-angle approximation. Near a downward equilibrium, γ < √(g/ℓ) gives underdamping; γ = √(g/ℓ) is critical damping; and γ > √(g/ℓ) gives overdamping. Here √(g/ℓ) ≈ 3.132 s⁻¹. These labels describe the local linearized dynamics.

E/m = ½ℓ²ω² + gℓ(1 − cos θ), and d(E/m)/dt = −2γℓ²ω². Damped trajectories approach downward equilibria at θ = 2nπ. An exactly upright, stationary pendulum is an unstable equilibrium. For rotation, θ is not wrapped at ±π; the horizontal view expands to keep the path visible.

Integration: fourth-order Runge–Kutta, fixed time step 1/240 s. Energy conservation at γ = 0 is approximate. Up to 24,000 trajectory samples are retained (about 200 simulation seconds); older segments then disappear. Playback pauses when the tab is hidden. All code is embedded; no internet connection is needed.
