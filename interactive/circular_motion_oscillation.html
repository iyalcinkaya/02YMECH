Tip: Drag the amber point around the circle or move the timeline slider. Increase the angular frequency to make the oscillation faster. Change the amplitude to change the radius of the circle and the maximum displacement.

Why this is simple harmonic motion. For uniform circular motion, the angle increases linearly: θ(t) = ωt + φ. Its horizontal coordinate is x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ). Differentiating twice gives d²x/dt² = −ω²x — the defining equation of simple harmonic motion. The particle on the circle and the oscillator are a mathematical construction: they need not be the same physical object.
