\section*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The time allocated for the exam is \underline{90 minutes}.
    \item The maximum score for the test is \underline{4 points} and each question is worth \underline{1 point}.
    \item During the exam, you \underline{are not} allowed to use any class materials or ask your neighbor for help.
    \item Calculators and use of any class materials \underline{are not} allowed.
    \item Pencils and erasable pens \underline{are not} allowed.
    \item Write your answers \underline{legibly}. Illegible answers will not be scored.
    \item \textbf{The grading of your exams will be based on the answers you have written on the five stapled sheets of paper you are holding in your hand.}

\end{enumerate}
